Common Questions

Frequently Asked

What is the minimum order quantity?

Our MOQ is 50 kg per product. For first-time buyers, we recommend starting with a sample order of 1–5 kg before placing bulk orders.

Do you provide lab test reports?

Yes. Every batch comes with a NABL certified Certificate of Analysis (COA) covering heavy metals, pesticide residue, microbial count and purity.

What shipping options do you offer?

We offer air freight for small/urgent orders and sea freight (LCL/FCL) for bulk shipments. We work with experienced freight forwarders for smooth delivery.

What documents do you provide?

We provide: Certificate of Analysis, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Phytosanitary Certificate, and Bill of Lading. Custom documentation on request.

Can you do custom packaging?

Yes. We can provide products in PP bags, HDPE drums, or custom packaging as per buyer requirements. Private label options available for bulk orders.

How long does delivery take?

Production and testing takes 7–14 days. Air freight to USA/Australia takes 3–5 days. Sea freight takes 20–35 days depending on destination port.
