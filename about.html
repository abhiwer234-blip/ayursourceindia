Our Story

Why We Started

India has been the world's herbal capital for millennia. From ancient Ayurvedic texts to modern wellness research, herbs like Amla, Neem and Fitkari have stood the test of time. Yet, many global buyers struggle to find a reliable, quality-conscious Indian supplier they can trust.

AyurSource India was founded to bridge this gap. We saw a clear opportunity — international distributors and wholesalers needed a professional, transparent and responsive Indian herbal exporter who understands both quality standards and global export requirements.

Based in Ghaziabad, in the heart of North India's trading hub, we are well-positioned to source the finest quality herbs and deliver them to markets across the USA, Australia, UK, Canada and beyond.

Every order we ship represents our commitment — to quality, to consistency, and to building long-term partnerships with our global buyers.
